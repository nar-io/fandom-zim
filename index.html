Fandom wikis, offline

Prebuilt ZIM archives of Fandom wikis for LocalWiki, the offline ZIM reader for KOReader. Drop one on your e-reader and read it with Wi-Fi off.

How to use: open a wiki below, download the _full.zim (with pictures) or _no_image.zim (text only), copy it into your ZIM folder — the default is /mnt/us/wikipedia — and restart KOReader. Each archive is a single file unless it is over 4 GB, in which case it ships split into .zimaa, .zimab… parts that must all be copied.

These are prebuilt archives, not a service. No warranty — back up your KOReader settings folder before you start.
